04 / Research & publications

Research with
real-world relevance.

Scientific work at the intersection of computer science, machine learning, and medical image analysis.

Peer-reviewed researchJCSI · 2025 · pp. 410–417

Comparative analysis of methods for identifying tree structures of coronary vessels

Kacper Liżewski, Małgorzata Charytanowicz

This paper compares machine learning models and a filter-based method for semantic segmentation of coronary vessels in coronary angiographic images. The study evaluates multiple approaches to medical image segmentation and reports FR-UNet as the best-performing model among those tested, with an accuracy of 97.7%.
